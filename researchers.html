Researchers & Professors

Professors and lead researchers coordinating the Monte Carlo Modelling Expert Group (MCMEG), guiding research in radiation transport, dosimetry, microdosimetry, and radiobiology.

Telma C. F. Fonseca

Founder & Coordinator of MCMEG | Associate Professor, Department of Nuclear Engineering (DEN), UFMG

Associate Professor at DEN/UFMG and supervisor in the Graduate Program in Nuclear Sciences and Techniques (PCTN/UFMG). Specializes in computational modeling, Monte Carlo radiation transport simulation, internal dosimetry, radiobiology, and GPU-accelerated methods applied to medical physics and radiation protection.

Students & Researchers

Postdoctoral researchers, graduate, and undergraduate students carrying out research within MCMEG, working on Monte Carlo modelling and simulation applied to radiation protection and dosimetry.

Asif Amin

PhD Student in Medical Physics, Nuclear Engineering Department, UFMG

PhD researcher in medical physics at UFMG, under the supervision of Prof. Adriano Sabino and Prof. Telma Fonseca. Holds a master's degree in theoretical nuclear physics, specializing in modelling nuclear reaction cross sections with the TALYS code. Current research applies Monte Carlo simulations (TOPAS–nBio) to study dose enhancement effects of gold nanoparticles in radiation dosimetry, with broader experience in radiation transport simulation, treatment planning, and computational dosimetry.
