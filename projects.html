Redes Temáticas CNPq Pepe Mujica Program Start: October 2026

TBI Latin American Network

Rede Latino-Americana para Modelagem, Calibração, Dosimetria Avançada e Inteligência Artificial Aplicadas à Irradiação Corporal Total (TBI)

Collaborative network uniting institutions from Argentina, Brazil, Chile, Mexico, and Peru to develop an integrated platform for computational modeling, Monte Carlo dosimetry, AI-based dose parameter prediction, and customized 3D-printed anthropomorphic phantoms.

AI & Oncology Open Data Start: October 2025

REMPTO — AI in Oncology

Inteligência Artificial aplicada à Oncologia (Instituto Mário Penna & MCMEG/UFMG) · rempto.org.br

Development of deep learning and CNN models for oncology. First stage includes curation of histopathology datasets from Instituto Mário Penna and public NIH ovarian cancer databases, with an open repository at rempto-ai.github.io.

Monte Carlo SAF Open Science Release: July 2026

Internal Dosimetry — SAF Database

Specific Absorbed Fractions (SAFs) generated via Monte Carlo simulations at UFMG

Computational repository of Specific Absorbed Fractions and organ S-values calculated from Monte Carlo simulations (MCNP, PHITS, TOPAS) using reference computational phantoms, inspired by OpenDose.org.

MCMEG Intercomparison Monte Carlo Simulations Deadline: December 2026

3rd Intercomparison Exercise of MCMEG (2026)

Monte Carlo modelling of a clinical diagnostic X-ray system (VMI Pulsar 800 Plus) at CDTN/CNEN, Brazil

Collaborative intercomparison exercise comparing independent Monte Carlo simulations with experimental measurements of X-ray beam attenuation, air kerma, Half-Value Layers (HVLs), beam homogeneity, backscatter, and radiological response of water and resin materials.
