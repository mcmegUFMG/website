3rd Intercomparison Exercise of MCMEG (2026)

The Monte Carlo Modelling Expert Group (MCMEG) is pleased to announce the 3rd MCMEG Intercomparison Exercise, focused this year on the Monte Carlo modelling of a clinical diagnostic X-ray system (VMI Pulsar 800 Plus) at the Calibration and Dosimetry Laboratory of CDTN/CNEN, Brazil.

The exercise aims to compare independent Monte Carlo simulations with experimental measurements, including:

  • 🔹 X-ray beam attenuation
  • 🔹 Air kerma
  • 🔹 Half-Value Layers (HVLs)
  • 🔹 Beam homogeneity
  • 🔹 Backscatter
  • 🔹 Radiological response of water and resin materials

Participants are invited to use the Monte Carlo code of their choice and contribute their results to this collaborative intercomparison.

The results from participating groups will be compiled, compared, and discussed, with the goal of developing a joint manuscript for submission to an international peer-reviewed journal.

📎 The complete exercise description, experimental conditions, and simulation cases are available in the PDF attached to this post.

  • ✅ Compare simulation results with experimental measurements
  • ✅ Exchange experience with researchers from different institutions and countries
  • ✅ Contribute to a collaborative scientific publication

Researchers, professors, students, programmers, medical physicists, physicists, engineers, and Monte Carlo users are all very welcome to participate.
