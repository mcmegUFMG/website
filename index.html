Monte Carlo Modelling Expert Group

MCMEG

An expert research network established in 2016, MCMEG specializes in Monte Carlo radiation transport modelling and simulation applied to radiation protection, medical physics, and dosimetry. Hosted at the Post-Graduate Program in Nuclear Sciences and Techniques of the Universidade Federal de Minas Gerais (PCTN/UFMG) in Belo Horizonte, Brazil, the group brings together professors, researchers, and students from partner institutions across Brazil and abroad to advance computational methods, intercomparison exercises, and open scientific tools.
