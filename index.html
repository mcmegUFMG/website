Monte Carlo Modelling Expert Group

MCMEG.UFMG

An expert network group, established in 2016, specialized in Monte Carlo radiation transport modelling and simulation applied to the radiation protection and dosimetry research fields. Hosted at the Post-Graduate Program in Nuclear Sciences and Techniques, UFMG, Belo Horizonte, Brazil.
