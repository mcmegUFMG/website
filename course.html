Introduction to the Monte Carlo Method

A free, fully online extension course covering computational modelling and simulation with the Monte Carlo method, with emphasis on applications in nuclear engineering and radiation sciences. The course gives participants basic knowledge of the Monte Carlo Method and the main codes used for radiation transport simulation.

Format

100% online and free, organized into modules, delivered as recorded video lessons (2021), in Portuguese.

Instructors

  • Telma C. F. Fonseca
  • Bruno Melo Mendes
  • Jhonny Benavente
  • Lucas Paixão Reis
