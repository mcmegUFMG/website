Internal Dosimetry — Specific Absorbed Fractions (SAF)

MCMEG presents an open computational repository of Specific Absorbed Fractions (SAFs) generated through Monte Carlo radiation transport simulations. Developed by master's and doctoral researchers at UFMG, this initiative provides validated dosimetric data, organ S-values, and computational phantom simulation results for radiation protection, medical physics, and theranostic applications.

Specific Absorbed Fractions (SAFs)

Specific Absorbed Fractions (SAFs) are calculated directly from Monte Carlo simulations. Absorbed doses to target organs and tissues are converted to SAFs by dividing by the initial particle energy (expressed in Joules) and by the number of simulated primary particles.

To display SAFs in our interactive portal, select a computational model from the left panel, then choose a source organ, target organ, and particle type (photons, electrons, etc.).

SAFs are organized within a relational database as a function of model parameters, source and target regions, particle type, energy spectrum, and total simulated histories. Each source, target, and particle species is identified with a unique ID linking to detailed anatomical model specifications (geometry, volume, and tissue mass). Every SAF dataset is fully traceable with complete provenance metadata, including data provider, Monte Carlo code version (MCNP, PHITS, TOPAS), contributor contact, and publication date.
